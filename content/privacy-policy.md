This website is run by ARIS (arisweare.com). We keep things simple: the site itself does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it has no customer accounts.

## What we collect

When you order or contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive what you send us, such as your name, phone number, delivery address and the items you want. We use it only to process your order, arrange delivery and handle returns.

## WhatsApp

Orders are placed through WhatsApp, a service run by Meta. When you message us there, WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies.

## How long we keep it

We keep order details for as long as we need them to fulfil the order and to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

## Who we share it with

We share your details only with the courier delivering your order, and with anyone we are legally required to share them with. We never sell your data.

## Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Write to hi@arisweare.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
